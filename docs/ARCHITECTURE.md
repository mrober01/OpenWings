# OpenWings — architecture

How the game is put together. Module contracts (units, aircraft meshes, HUD state, cockpit, axis/unit
conventions) are in `docs/INTERFACES.md`.

## Design decisions (and why)

| Area | Decision | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| World | `world/Heightfield.js`: 40 km Nordic island on a 512² grid, generated once. Mesh **and** collision sample the same grid with the same triangle split | No "floating/sinking" mismatch between what you see and what you hit. Pure JS, so it runs in node tests |
| Airfields | 3 sites auto-picked by flatness + clear approaches, then flattened: `home`, `civil`, `enemy`. City near the civil field | Robust to changing the terrain seed/shape |
| Flight model | Per-unit-mass forces, real g. Lift/drag from normalised CL(α); G-command fly-by-wire pitch loop; corner speed and sustained turn emerge from `vStall`, `gMax`, `kn`, `twr` | Believable energy fighting, yet "stick centred = holds flight path" which is what makes touch flying pleasant |
| Assist | Setting `assist`: α-limiter (no stalls), bank-to-turn pull compensation, wings auto-level near level, auto wheel-brake at idle | Phone playability. Off = raw aeroplane |
| Throttle | One axis 0..1; for afterburner aircraft the top ~8 % is the AB detent | One slider on touch, no extra button |
| AI | Enemies fly the **same** FlightModel through a stick-input autopilot (`engine/AIPilot.js`) | Fair fights; they bleed energy and can be out-flown |
| Rendering | Custom shaders for sky / terrain / water / clouds sharing one atmosphere (fog) function; aircraft are one vertex-coloured MeshStandardMaterial mesh each (colour ×2.3 because the scene lights are balanced for the terrain shader; no env map yet); 3D cockpit in a second scene drawn after a depth clear, camera-relative | Scenic look at phone cost; cockpit needs a tiny near plane that would wreck world depth precision |
| HUD | One full-screen 2D canvas (`ui/Hud.js`), world-conformal symbology (pitch ladder, flight-path marker, target boxes, lead pipper, ILS) | Classic fighter feel; cheap; identical on phone and desktop |
| Particles | One instanced-quad batch with premultiplied alpha (smoke + additive fire in one draw call), ring-buffer pool | Draw calls / GC |
| Audio | Procedural WebAudio (`engine/Audio.js`), unlocked on the FLY tap | No assets |

## Real-city maps

`nyc`, `paris`, `london`, `rio` are 1:1 slices of real places on the same 40 km grid.

- **Terrain**: `Maps.realShape()` reads the baked class grid (0 water · 1 land · 2 park · 3 woods) and a chamfer
  distance-to-shore field: water deepens off the coast (river maps get a deep channel at once so the water shader
  does not turn a two-cell river into surf), land rises inland, plus hand-placed hills (`hill`, `ridge`) or real SRTM
  heights (`R.elevAt`, Catmull-Rom) sharpened at named peaks. The last ~0.8 km fall into the sea like every map.
- **Sites**: `Heightfield.placeRealSites()` takes the longest OSM runway of each airport in `map.sites.fields`
  (home / civil / enemy) instead of searching for flat ground; the strip is still a 2600 m game runway.
- **Town**: `map.urban` districts (`zones`) give a street-grid angle (constant, or a patchwork of Voronoi cells,
  baked to a 512² texture both the terrain shader and the placer read), town ground (`urbanGround`), a building
  budget share and an optional `fill` rule. `Environment.buildCity` places towers by district, then `fillBlocks`
  builds out whole blocks nearest the centre. Buildings may be rotated: every building carries `ca`/`sa` (footprint
  frame) and `q`; collision (`buildingAt`, `footDist`), shadows and damage all use them.
- **Landmarks**: `Landmarks.buildLandmarks()` runs before the town so footprints stay clear. Colliders are building
  objects with `style: 'landmark'` and infinite hp (solid, never break); bridges/lattices skip the baked shadow.
- **Menu**: the menu shows before the 3D backdrop is built (`UI.queueBackdrop`); map / time / graphics clicks rebuild
  the backdrop ~350 ms after the last click (MAP reads LOADING meanwhile) and the MAP option shows a pre-rendered
  top-down thumbnail at once (`src/ui/mapThumbs.js`, GENERATED by `node tools/thumbs.mjs` — rerun after map changes).
  `Game.ensureEnv` builds the new world before disposing the old one so shader programs are reused; FLY onto a world
  that isn't built yet shows the loading screen.


## Carrier ops

Every map has a carrier (`hf.carrier`, `env.carrier`), placed in deep water ~9 km from the home field (or just past the
world edge on inland maps). The pacific map (`carrierHome`) makes it the home base (`Game.homeBase()`).

- **Deck**: `Heightfield.ground()` checks `carrier.onDeck()` first and returns `{ y: DECK_Y, runway: true, carrier }`, so
  landing, rolling, rearming and shadows all just work. Arriving at the deck footprint more than 1.2 m below deck level is
  a crash (`'ship'`: ramp strike). The island is a solid box (`carrier.solidAt`).
- **Navigation**: `carrier.landing` looks like an airfield (x, z, y, heading, dir, length, width) plus `oneWay`, `aim`
  (aim point from the lane start) and `glide` (3.5°). `Game.navFields` = airfields + carrier landing: NAV cycling,
  nearest field, HUD map, ILS (one-way, own aim/glide) and the autopilot all use it.
- **Catapult**: `Game.spotOnCat()` sets `fm.cat`; the FlightModel holds the jet until `cat.fired` (Game fires it after
  1 s at full power), then drives it down the 100 m stroke at constant g to ~1.55 × the flaps-down stall, nose strut
  raising the attitude, and the shuttle keeps it on the deck until the end.
- **Wires**: Game tracks the hook point's lane coordinate each frame; crossing a wire with the hook down and the wheels
  on (or within 0.5 m of) the deck sets `fm.arrest`, a kinematic run-out (constant deceleration, thrust ignored).
  Idle in the wire → cleared → rearm → re-spotted on cat 1. Rolling off the angled deck without a wire is a bolter.

## Autopilot

`Game.setAP(mode)` builds an `Autopilot`; each frame `apInput()` replaces the pilot's input with `ap.update(dt)` unless
the pilot moves the stick firmly (> 0.3 for 0.2 s), which disengages it. Navigation legs run an internal `AIPilot`
(waypoint + terrain avoidance); precise legs (final approach, zoom climb, dive attack) use `steerToward()` with the nose
offset by the current angle of attack so the *flight path* goes where it should. Landing: join a gate on the extended
centreline (above the terrain on the way), carrot-pursuit the localiser, glide slope with a terrain floor, flare
(runway) or fly into the deck (carrier), go around if it is off. Space: accelerate low, zoom at 78°, latches +
override, motors in pairs from 10 km, coast, pull out on reentry (F-22 ≈ 103 km, Su-35 ≈ 139 km).

## Rocket hacks

Locked pylon latches turn missile launches into motors burning on the rails (`p.hung`, Game.hangFire). The two wingtip
rounds on missile-armed jets are attitude thrusters: with the latches locked and the air thin (ρ < 0.35), Game sets
`fm.rcs = 1` and the FlightModel blends the stick into direct body-rate commands (grain `p.rcs`, ~45 s of use, refilled
on rearm). `Game.toggleMotors()` (U / MTR) vents every rail motor (burns pause, grain kept) and safes the RCS.
